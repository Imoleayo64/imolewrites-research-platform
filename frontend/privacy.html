Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. ImoleWrites is currently in early access — this policy reflects how the product works today, and will be updated as new features (like third-party integrations) are added.

What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). If you choose to add them, we also store optional profile details: institution, field of study, role, and bio.

What you create

Your projects, documents, citations, and any PDFs you upload are stored so you can access them later. If you invite a collaborator, they can see and edit the project you've shared with them, according to the role (editor or viewer) you assign.

AI features

Features like the AI Assistant, Humanizer, and Journal Recommendation send the text you provide to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. We don't control how Google processes that data beyond what's needed to return a response to you — see Google's own privacy policy for details on their handling.

Literature and citation lookups

When you search for papers or look up a citation by DOI, your search terms are sent to CrossRef's public API to retrieve results. CrossRef is a third-party service we don't control.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We don't share your documents with anyone except collaborators you explicitly invite. We currently have no payment processor connected, so we don't collect or store any payment information.

Your control over your data

You can update your profile information or delete your account entirely at any time from Settings → Security. Deleting your account permanently removes your projects, citations, and uploaded files.

Changes to this policy

As ImoleWrites adds new features — collaboration tools, reference-manager integrations, or a payment system — this policy will be updated to reflect them. We'll note the "last updated" date above when that happens.

Questions

Reach out any time at support@imolewrites.com.
